THE ONE CONDITION, STATED PLAINLY

Activating S Protocol on an existing S Invoice workspace starts on a paid S Protocol plan, and the workspace owner is the one who does it. Creating a free S Protocol account instead is a separate signup with a fresh, empty workspace.

USING BOTH

Can I use both products at once?

Yes. A workspace with both keeps its S Invoice side working exactly as before. Customers are shared across both; invoices raised against dispatched jobs stay on the S Protocol side, so your invoicing list doesn’t fill up with operational paperwork.
