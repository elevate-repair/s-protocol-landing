THE FREE PLAN

How Free works.

  • One-time, not monthly

    Free is an allowance for the business: 5 jobs, 5 invoices and 5 payments on S Protocol; 3 invoices and 3 payments on S Invoice. Deleting a document doesn’t give it back.

  • The real product

    Every S Protocol feature is unlocked on Free, and S Invoice Free is the same app Pro uses. It’s not a stripped-down demo, and there’s no Pro trial countdown.

  • Yours to keep

    Hit the limit or stop paying, and everything you created stays readable.
