ABOUT

Built by people running the jobs.

S Protocol and S Invoice are made by Elevate Repair LLC. The first workspace was an appliance repair business — and it still runs on this.

THE STORY

Made by a service business, for service businesses.

Every workflow here started as a problem somebody had on a Tuesday morning: a technician stuck without the history, an invoice nobody chased, a job assigned twice. That’s why the dispatch board looks the way it does — and why S Invoice exists separately for people who only need the paperwork.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Four policies we hold ourselves to.

  1. 01

    One price for the business.

    S Protocol Pro is $24.99/mo with no per-seat fees and no team-member limit.

  2. 02

    Free means the real product.

    Free is the same product Pro uses. No stripped-down demo, no trial countdown.

  3. 03

    Your records are never held hostage.

    If billing lapses, everything you created stays readable.

  4. 04

    Honest labels.

    Integrations are marked available with setup or by request. There’s no Android app, and we won’t claim one until there is.
