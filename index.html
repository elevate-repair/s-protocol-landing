Questions worth answering properly

The things people ask before signing up.

What happens when I hit the Free limit?

You stop being able to create new invoices of that kind until you upgrade. Nothing is hidden, locked or deleted — every invoice, payment and customer you already have stays fully readable. The allowance is one-time per business, not monthly, and deleting a document does not give it back.

Can I move from S Invoice to S Protocol later?

Yes, and it is not a migration. S Protocol is activated on the same workspace, so your customers, invoices, estimates, company details and invoice numbering are simply still there. The owner starts it from Settings, and that first activation is on a paid S Protocol plan. A free S Protocol account is instead a separate signup with an empty workspace.

Is there a free trial of Pro?

No. There is a Free plan with a real allowance instead, and it uses the same product Pro does — not a stripped demo. We would rather you test the real thing than race a countdown.

Do I pay per technician?

No. S Protocol Pro has no team-member limit and no per-seat charge. Add the dispatchers and technicians the business actually employs.

What if I stop paying?

Your data is never held hostage. If a subscription lapses or is cancelled, you keep read access to everything and drop back to Free limits for creating new records. Cancelling mid-period keeps Pro running until the period you paid for ends.

Which currencies can I invoice in?

S Invoice documents can be USD, CAD, EUR or GBP, and online card payments work in all four. The currency is stamped when the document is created and never converted afterwards. In-person card payments are USD only for now.

Do both products work on iPhone?

Yes — both run on the web and on iPhone. There is no Android app, and we will not claim one until there is.

Can I use both products at once?

Yes. A workspace with both keeps its S Invoice side working exactly as before. Customers are shared across both; invoices raised against dispatched jobs stay on the S Protocol side so your invoicing list does not fill up with operational paperwork.
